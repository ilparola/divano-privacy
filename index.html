Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Divano is a music player for Navidrome and other Subsonic-compatible servers, for Android TV and Android phones. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What Divano collects

Nothing. Divano has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer does not receive any information about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your device

  • The address of your server and your username.
  • An authentication token (a salted hash, as defined by the Subsonic API). Your password is never stored.
  • Your preferences: streaming quality, albums per row, language.

This information is kept only in the app's private storage on your device and is deleted when you sign out or uninstall the app.

Who Divano talks to

Only the server you enter. Divano sends requests to it to browse your library, play music, show cover art and report what you listen to ("scrobbling"), which your server may forward to services you have configured yourself, such as Last.fm or ListenBrainz. With an http:// address this traffic is not encrypted; use an https:// address to encrypt it.

Children

Divano is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
